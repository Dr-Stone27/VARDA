# November–December 2026 Execution Plan

**Status:** Working plan; adjust based on findings and founder capacity.

| Window | Workstream | Required output | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 6–9 | First-principles reset | Varda thesis, opportunity hypotheses, unknowns and research agenda | Founder agrees reset accurately separates source facts from hypotheses |
| Nov 10–16 | Market/technology intelligence | Landscape, substitutes and competitor map | Major alternatives and capability boundaries understood for H1–H3 |
| Nov 10–20 | Customer discovery | Prospect briefs and 5–8 substantive conversations (target) | Repeated patterns, contradictions and buying process recorded |
| Nov 21–25 | Opportunity synthesis | Segment/problem comparison and recommendation | One candidate clearly outranks alternatives or evidence says continue discovery |
| Nov 26–30 | Solution definition | Solution brief, workflow map, technical approach, pilot plan | One bounded customer/workflow/outcome selected with test criteria |
| Dec 1–10 | Build/configure | Working pilot or prototype | Pilot passes basic functional and safety checks on representative cases |
| Dec 11–20 | Test and iterate | Evaluation results, failure log, user feedback | Baseline comparison and key limitations documented |
| Dec 21–31 | Evidence and decision | Case study, commercial hypothesis and build/pivot/stop recommendation | Decision is supported by observed customer and technical evidence |

## Operating principle

The calendar is a planning constraint, not a reason to force a false positive. If no opportunity passes the gate by late November, the correct December output may be a targeted test or a documented decision not to build the proposed solution.
