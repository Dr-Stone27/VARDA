# Initial Working Session — Agenda

**Goal:** Complete the reset and agree the discovery programme, not choose a product by intuition.

1. Validate the reconstruction of the original Varda thesis.
2. Correct missing or misrepresented context from the founder.
3. Challenge the refined problem statement.
4. Confirm H1, H2, H3 and H0 as the initial research set, or revise them.
5. Select up to three segments for comparable discovery.
6. Agree what evidence would make us pursue, defer or reject each hypothesis.
7. Approve the prospect selection criteria and discovery outreach approach.
8. Assign immediate research tasks and update the decision log.

## The session is complete when

- The founder agrees the reset reflects the original intent without prematurely preserving the old solution.
- Each hypothesis has explicit questions and disconfirming evidence.
- The customer discovery sample and first prospect list are defined.
- No unsupported market or customer claim is presented as fact.
