# Venture Critical Review

1. Restate the decision and the actual problem being addressed.
2. Separate problem, customer, intervention, product, business model and distribution.
3. Summarise supporting evidence with dates and confidence.
4. Present the strongest counterargument and the null hypothesis.
5. Identify hidden assumptions, dependencies, incentives and second-order effects.
6. Compare at least one credible alternative to the proposed direction.
7. Distinguish reversible from costly/irreversible decisions.
8. Recommend the smallest test that could change the decision.
9. Do not use arbitrary scoring or claim certainty beyond the evidence.
10. Update the decision log only when the founder explicitly makes or approves a decision; otherwise label the recommendation as proposed.
