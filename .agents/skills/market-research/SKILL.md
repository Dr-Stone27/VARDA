# Market Research for Varda

1. Translate the question into explicit claims and decision relevance before searching.
2. Prefer primary sources: official product documentation, pricing pages, technical documentation, filings, regulators, standards bodies, original research and direct company announcements.
3. Use independent reporting and customer discussions to triangulate, not replace primary evidence.
4. Record the publication date, event date, geography, sample, method and definition for statistics.
5. Separate vendor marketing claims from independently measured results.
6. Compare products by the customer job, workflow coverage, integration model, permissions, evaluation, observability, target buyer, deployment burden and pricing where available.
7. Identify substitutes, including internal engineering, manual work, consultants, incumbent platforms and doing nothing.
8. Search for disconfirming evidence and cases where the proposed category is not a problem.
9. Do not infer that market growth proves Varda demand.
10. Update `docs/01_market/evidence_register.md` and `docs/04_operations/research_log.md` with citations and caveats.
11. End with implications for H1, H2, H3 and H0, confidence, unknowns and the next customer test.
