**Working name:** Varda (not yet a settled brand)

## Purpose

This repository is the working knowledge base for investigating and potentially building Varda. It is designed to preserve the original thinking, distinguish evidence from assumptions, support customer discovery, and create a controlled transition from opportunity selection to solution design and December proof.

Varda is not yet defined as a software product, consultancy, platform, vertical, or brand. Those are possible choices to investigate rather than settled facts.

## Current working thesis (not validated)

Businesses may be gaining access to increasingly capable AI systems faster than they are developing the workflows, context, integration, authority boundaries, evaluation and operational ownership required to use those systems reliably and profitably.

Varda will investigate whether a specific, urgent and economically meaningful problem exists within that broad gap, for whom it is most acute, and what intervention customers will pay for.

## Working rules

- Evidence outranks narrative quality.
- Customer statements are evidence of reported experience, not automatically proof of prevalence or willingness to pay.
- Distinguish facts, reported observations, inferences, assumptions and unknowns.
- Preserve disagreement and uncertainty; do not silently reconcile conflicting sources.
- Keep the original documents intact.
- No industry, persona, business model, price or product is considered final until supported by evidence and recorded in the decision log.
- A document, prototype or interview count is not success by itself. Progress means reducing important uncertainty or producing observed customer value.

## Immediate milestone

Complete the first-principles reset and design the discovery programme. The next milestone is a defensible choice of one customer/problem/workflow to solve, not a polished brand or an expansive platform roadmap.
